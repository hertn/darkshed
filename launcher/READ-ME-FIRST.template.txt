DarkShed - READ ME FIRST
========================

DarkShed is a photo editor that runs in your web browser. Your photos never
leave your device. This folder runs a tiny program on your computer that opens
DarkShed and keeps your presets, so you can use the same presets on your phone
or tablet. You do not need to install anything else.


1. START IT
-----------
{{START_STEPS}}

A window opens and your browser opens DarkShed. The window shows two
addresses:

  - the address for this computer, and
  - the address for your phone or tablet (it starts with http://192.168...).

Keep that window open while you use DarkShed. Close it to stop DarkShed.

{{FIRST_RUN_NOTE}}


2. USE IT ON YOUR PHONE OR TABLET
---------------------------------
1. Your computer must be on, DarkShed must be running (step 1, or step 3
   below), and the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Open the phone address from the window in your phone's browser
   (Safari on iPhone and iPad).
3. On iPhone and iPad you can tap Share > Add to Home Screen to get an app icon.

Your presets and LUTs are saved in the "data" folder in this folder and are
shared by every device that opens DarkShed from the SAME address. So on your
computer, also use the address that starts with http://192.168... (not
"localhost"), otherwise the computer and the phone do not share presets.

Photos and settings are NOT shared: each device keeps its own.

The first time, your computer may ask whether to allow DarkShed (or "node")
to accept network connections. Choose Allow, or your phone cannot reach it.

If the address changes later (your router restarted), look at the window
again. It always shows the current one.


3. OPTIONAL: KEEP IT RUNNING IN THE BACKGROUND
----------------------------------------------
If you do not want to leave the window open, you can set DarkShed to start by
itself every time you log in to this computer, with no window.

  {{BG_STEPS}}

It asks for an optional sync password, then tells you the address.
Things to know:

  - The computer must be awake. If it sleeps or is shut down, your phone
    cannot reach DarkShed until it wakes up.
  - It uses almost no power or memory.
  - Without a password, anyone on your Wi-Fi who opens the address could see or
    change your presets (they cannot reach any photos, because photos are never
    stored by DarkShed). If you share your Wi-Fi with people you do not trust,
    set a password. Then enter the same password on each device under
    Menu > Sync & settings > Token.
  - If you move or rename this folder later, run the "run in background" step
    again.


4. STOP AND UNINSTALL
---------------------
Only used it by double-clicking Start? Close the window. To remove DarkShed,
delete this folder. Copy the "data" folder first if you want to keep your
presets.

Used "run in background"? First do this:

  {{STOP_STEPS}}

Then delete this folder. If you deleted the folder first, remove the leftover
start-up entry by hand:

{{MANUAL_REMOVE}}

DarkShed also keeps a crash-recovery copy of the photos you open, inside each
browser (not in this folder). To clear it, open DarkShed > Menu >
Sync & settings > "Delete all stored copies", or clear the site data for
the address in your browser.


5. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
--------------------------
- The window closes right away: open it from a terminal/command prompt to see
  the message, or look at data/darkshed.log if you use the background mode.
- "Address already in use": another program uses the port. DarkShed picks the
  next free one and shows it in the window.
- Your phone cannot connect: check it is on the same Wi-Fi (not guest or
  mobile data), that the window or background mode is running, and that the
  firewall allowed it (see step 2).
- More help and the source code: https://github.com/hertn/darkshed
